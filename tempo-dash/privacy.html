Tempo Dash Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how Tempo Dash: One Tap Arcade (the "Game"), made by Lucito Labs, handles information. It applies to the Game on Android.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal or usage data. The Game has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and makes no network requests.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress on your device: scores, in-game currency, unlocked items, missions, settings and simple play statistics such as the number of runs played. This data stays in the Game's private storage and is never sent to us.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this save file in your device backup to your own Google account. That backup is handled by Google under your account settings, and we cannot access it. Uninstalling the Game deletes the save file from your device.

Permissions

The Game uses the vibration permission for haptic feedback. You can turn vibration off in the Game's settings.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that processes data, such as advertising, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
