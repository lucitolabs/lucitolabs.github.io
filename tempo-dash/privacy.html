Tempo Dash Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how Tempo Dash: One Tap Arcade (the "Game"), made by Lucito Labs, handles information. It applies to the Game on Android and iOS.

Information we collect

Lucito Labs does not run its own servers and does not collect personal data directly. The Game has no user accounts and no analytics of its own. The only data that leaves your device is the data processed by our advertising partner, described below.

Advertising

The Game shows ads served by Google AdMob, including ads between runs and optional rewarded ads (for example, watching an ad to continue a run). To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following data with Google:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app and SDK performance
  • Device identifiers: on Android, the advertising ID and app set ID; on iOS, the identifier for vendors (IDFV) and, only if you allow tracking, the advertising identifier (IDFA)

This data is encrypted in transit. Where required by law, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from Privacy choices in the Game's settings. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Tracking on iOS

On iOS, the Game asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier (IDFA) is shared with Google for personalized ads. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared and you still see ads, which may be less relevant to you. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. The Game also supports Apple's SKAdNetwork, which lets advertisers measure ad results without identifying you.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress on your device: scores, in-game currency, unlocked items, missions, settings and simple play statistics such as the number of runs played. This data stays in the Game's private storage and is never sent to us.

If backup is turned on for your device, the save file may be included in your device backup: on Android, to your own Google account; on iOS, to your own iCloud account. Those backups are handled by Google or Apple under your account settings, and we cannot access them. Uninstalling the Game deletes the save file from your device.

Permissions

On Android, the Game uses the vibration permission for haptic feedback. On iOS, the only permission the Game requests is the tracking permission described above. You can turn vibration off in the Game's settings on both platforms.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how the Game processes data, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
