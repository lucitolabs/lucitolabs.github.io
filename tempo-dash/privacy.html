Tempo Dash Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This policy explains how Tempo Dash: One Tap Arcade (the "Game"), made by Lucito Labs, handles information. It applies to the Game on Android and iOS.

Information we collect

Lucito Labs does not run its own servers and the Game has no user accounts. Data leaves your device only through two Google services: crash reports and usage statistics on Android (Firebase), and ads (AdMob), both described below.

Crash reports and usage statistics (Android)

On Android, the Game uses Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google, to find and fix crashes and to understand how the Game is played so we can balance it. The iOS version does not use them. When the Game first starts, it creates a random installation ID; it is not your name, email or advertising ID, and it is not linked to them. Crash reports carry that ID; usage statistics carry it only while Analytics may store information on your device (see below). The following data is sent to Google:

  • Crash and error reports: where in the Game's code the problem happened, a short log of recent in-game events (such as which mode you were playing and the speed level), the app version, device model, operating system version and the device's state at the time (for example free memory and orientation)
  • Gameplay events: runs started and ended (mode, duration, score, highest speed level, bosses passed), portals taken, cards picked, missions completed, opening the How to play guide, shop purchases made with in-game currency, and whether an ad was shown or watched to the end
  • Information collected automatically by Google Analytics: app opens, session length, app version, device model, operating system, language, the country estimated from your IP address, and the Firebase app instance and installation IDs

This data is encrypted in transit and is used only to improve the Game. It is not used for advertising: Analytics does not collect your advertising ID or Android ID, never shares advertising signals, and is not linked to AdMob. Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days; Analytics keeps event data for 2 months and data tied to the installation, such as its identifiers, for 14 months after you last play. Where consent is required by law, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Analytics stores identifiers on your device and receives the installation ID only if you allow storing and accessing information on your device in the consent form; without that permission it sends only events without identifiers or cookies, which Google uses in aggregate. You can change this from Privacy choices in the Game's settings. You can stop sending crash and error reports with the Crash reports switch, and usage statistics with the Usage data switch, at any time in the Game's settings on Android. Learn how Google handles Firebase data at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

If you use Report a problem in the Game's settings, the email it prepares contains your installation ID and simple play statistics, so we can match your report with crash reports. You can ask us to delete that data at any time, as described under Deleting your data.

Advertising

The Game shows ads served by Google AdMob, including ads between runs and optional rewarded ads (for example, watching an ad to continue a run). To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following data with Google:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app and SDK performance
  • Device identifiers: on Android, the advertising ID and app set ID; on iOS, the identifier for vendors (IDFV) and, only if you allow tracking, the advertising identifier (IDFA)

This data is encrypted in transit. Where required by law, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from Privacy choices in the Game's settings. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Tracking on iOS

On iOS, the Game asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier (IDFA) is shared with Google for personalized ads. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared and you still see ads, which may be less relevant to you. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. The Game also supports Apple's SKAdNetwork, which lets advertisers measure ad results without identifying you.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress on your device: scores, in-game currency, unlocked items, missions, settings and simple play statistics such as the number of runs played. This data stays in the Game's private storage and is never sent to us.

If backup is turned on for your device, the save file may be included in your device backup: on Android, to your own Google account; on iOS, to your own iCloud account. Those backups are handled by Google or Apple under your account settings, and we cannot access them. Uninstalling the Game deletes the save file from your device.

Deleting your data

Tempo Dash: One Tap Arcade by Lucito Labs has no accounts, so your data is identified only by the Game's random installation ID. To have the crash reports and usage data linked to that ID deleted:

  1. In the Game, open Settings and tap Report a problem. Your email app opens with a message that already contains the line starting with "Install".
  2. Write "Delete my data" in the message and send it to lucitolabs@gmail.com without removing the Install line. If you can no longer open the Game, email us anyway and we will tell you what we can do.
  3. Within 30 days, we delete the crash reports from Firebase Crashlytics and the usage data from Google Analytics that are linked to that ID, and reply to confirm.

Reports and statistics that are already combined and no longer linked to any installation are not deleted. Advertising data is controlled by Google, not by us; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Your progress saved on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

Permissions

On Android, the Game uses the vibration permission for haptic feedback; the Google services above also use internet access, the advertising ID permission and Google Play's install referrer, which tells Analytics from which store page the Game was installed. On iOS, the only permission the Game requests is the tracking permission described above. You can turn vibration off in the Game's settings on both platforms.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how the Game processes data, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
